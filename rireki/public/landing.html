Everything between the trainee's CV and the client's decision

Built around how sending organizations actually work: many staff, many candidates, many Japanese clients, and strict expectations about privacy.

Import from DOCX or PDF

Upload the CV you already have. Rireki extracts names, katakana, education, work history and Japanese level, and shows you what to double-check.

Guided 履歴書 form

A 7-step form in the staff member's language that outputs a correct Japanese 履歴書, including 家族構成, 健康状態 and 志望動機.

One profile per person

A candidate code, original and katakana names, one CV, several self-introduction videos and internal documents, all in one place.

Share links, not attachments

Pick candidates, set a password, an expiry and whether download is allowed, and send one link. Single-candidate links work too.

View-only protection

Watermarked pages with the viewer's name, no files served, printing and copying blocked, content hidden when the window is inactive.

Know who looked

Views per candidate, per viewer and per day, video watch rate, and "Interested" marks sent back by the client.

How it works

Register and get your subdomain

yourcompany.rireki.app with one or more admins and as many staff users as you need.

Build your candidate database

Import files or fill the form, add videos, tag by job type and Japanese level.

Send a link and follow up

The client opens the link with a password, reviews candidates, marks interest. You see every view.

Made for the trust between you and your Japanese clients

Personal data of trainees is sensitive. Every link is scoped, expiring and traceable, and your team's access is controlled by roles.

  • Password or open links, expiry dates, view limits and email-domain restrictions
  • View-only or download-allowed, decided per link by the person who creates it
  • Admin and User roles, two-factor authentication, full audit log
  • Data hosted in Japan (Tokyo region), encrypted at rest and in transit
